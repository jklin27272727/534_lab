Cross-Asset Market Monitor

Live U.S. Treasury rates, FX and crypto pulled straight into the browser from free public APIs.

How this page works. It's one static index.html hosted on GitHub Pages. There's no server. When the page loads, your browser calls three public APIs directly: the U.S. Treasury Fiscal Data API, Frankfurter (ECB reference FX rates) and CoinGecko. If an API can't be reached, the page falls back to sample data and labels it as a sample.

Average interest rate on U.S. Treasury debt loading

Source: U.S. Treasury Fiscal Data, Average Interest Rates on U.S. Treasury Securities (monthly).
Reading it. Bills are the shortest maturities, so their rates follow the Fed policy rate almost one-for-one. Notes and bonds are locked in for years, so their average rate moves slowly as old low-coupon debt matures and gets refinanced at today's rates. When bill rates sit above the note average, refinancing pushes the government's interest bill up.
